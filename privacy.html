IGMate privacy policy

IGMate is a personal tool used only by its owner.

When the owner signs in with Google, IGMate accesses only the owner's own Google Drive, to save files there and organize them into folders. It does not collect, share, sell or send any data to anyone else, and it has no other users.

The owner can remove IGMate's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
